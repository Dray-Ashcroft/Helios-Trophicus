# ☀️ Helios Trophicus 

### *Energy Pyramid Simulator*

> **Solus EnergyOrb** is an interactive visualization of **energy flow, trophic levels, biomass, heat loss, and nutrient cycling** in an ecosystem.
>
🌱 **Ecosystem** · ☀️ **Energy Flow** · 🔬 **Trophic Levels**

**🔬 [Explore the Simulation](https://solus-en-orb.stark-kodex.workers.dev)**

---

## ✦ Features

**☀️ Solar Energy Input**  
Visualize PAR reaching producers and initiating energy flow.

**🔺 Trophic Levels**  
Explore energy transfer across four trophic levels.

**⚡ Energy, Heat & Biomass**  
Track changes in energy, heat loss, and biomass across trophic levels.

**♻️ Nutrient Cycling**  
Visualize detritus, decomposition, and nutrient return to producers.

**🧭 Guided Walkthrough**  
Follow a seven-step visualization of energy and matter movement.

**🔗 Food Chain View**  
Switch from the pyramid to a 3D food chain, **Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Eagle**, across five trophic levels. Arrows run from food to feeder, carry about 10% of the energy onward (100 → 0.01 rel. J, the same scale as the pyramid), and heat rises from every organism. Tap an organism to see what it eats, what eats it and how much energy reaches it, or walk through the chain one link at a time.

**🕸️ Food Web View**  
A third view links all the pyramid's organisms by who eats whom: producers, herbivores, carnivores and top predators across four trophic levels. Tap an organism to see only its links (the snake, for example, feeds at two trophic levels, and the leopard hunts the deer), or light up the food chain hidden inside the web.
