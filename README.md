## ✦ Three Views

**🔺 Energy Pyramid**: four trophic levels with 3D organisms. Tap the Sun for a seven-step walkthrough of energy and matter flow: PAR, GPP and NPP, the 10% transfer, heat loss, decomposition and nutrient return. Graph energy, heat or biomass across levels, and simulate mortality.

**🔗 Food Chain**: Grass → Grasshopper → Frog → Snake → Eagle. Energy falls 100 → 10 → 1 → 0.1 → 0.01 rel. J, with heat lost at every link. Step through it one link at a time.

**🕸️ Food Web**: 13 organisms, 15 feeding links. Tap an organism to see what it eats and what eats it, or highlight the food chain inside the web.
