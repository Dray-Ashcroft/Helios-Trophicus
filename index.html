GPP 100 − R 50 heat = NPP 50
GPP: energy fixed in photosynthesis. R: used in the producers' own respiration, lost as heat. NPP: stored as plant biomass — food for herbivores and decomposers.
NPP 50 → TL2 10 eaten + Litter 40
Herbivores can only eat what producers keep after respiration — the NPP, not the GPP. About 10 of it reaches TL2. The uneaten 40 becomes dead leaves and litter for the decomposers (step 6).
